Expert Tips

Daily Teeth Care Dos & Don'ts

Recommended Dos

  • Brush Twice Daily for 2 Minutes

    Brush in the morning and before bed using soft-bristled brushes to avoid damaging tooth enamel.

  • Floss Everyday without Fail

    Remove food residue and plaque built up in narrow areas between teeth where brush bristles cannot reach.

  • Use Fluoride Toothpaste

    Fluoride strengthens enamel and helps remineralize weak spots, preventing early cavity decay.

  • Visit Your Dentist Every 6 Months

    Preventive diagnostics and professional cleanings stop tartar buildup and spot oral issues early.

Important Don'ts

  • Don't Brush Too Aggressively

    Brushing with excessive force recedes sensitive gum tissue and wears down protective enamel.

  • Don't Use Teeth as Tools

    Avoid opening bottle caps, cutting tape, or chewing ice with your teeth. This frequently causes fractures and cracks.

  • Don't Consume Sugar Before Sleep

    Sugar nourishes oral bacteria. Brushing should always be the final step before you fall asleep.

  • Don't Ignore Bleeding Gums

    Bleeding gums is an early indicator of gingivitis. Don't wait for pain to manifest before booking checks.
