Frequently Asked Questions

We recommend visiting twice a year for routine check-ups and professional cleaning. Patients with specific concerns like gum disease or high cavity risk may benefit from more frequent visits — typically every 3–4 months. Early detection is key to preventing costly and complex treatments.

Absolutely. Our professional whitening uses clinically tested, dentist-grade peroxide gels applied under controlled conditions. Unlike over-the-counter products, our in-clinic treatment is customised to your enamel sensitivity level. Most patients experience no sensitivity, and results last 12–24 months with proper care.

Dental implants are designed to last a lifetime. The titanium implant itself typically lasts 25+ years, while the crown on top may need replacement after 10–15 years depending on wear. With proper brushing, flossing, and regular check-ups, your implant should function indefinitely — covered by our lifetime warranty.

Yes. We offer 0% interest EMI plans across all major treatments through our finance partners. Plans range from 3 to 24 months with no hidden charges. We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, UPI, and insurance reimbursements. Our patient coordinators will help you choose the best financial option.

Modern root canal treatment is no more uncomfortable than getting a filling. With highly effective local anaesthesia and our gentle rotary technique, most patients are surprised at how comfortable the procedure is. Post-treatment, mild soreness for 1–2 days is normal and easily managed with over-the-counter pain relief.

A Smile Makeover is a bespoke combination of cosmetic and restorative treatments tailored to your unique facial features, skin tone, and goals.
